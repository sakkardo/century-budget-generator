When a note is suggested: a line is more than 10% off its year-to-date budget, or more than 15% off this month's budget. Items the FA already explained last month come back as Previous notes, not new ones.

The portal reads the statement, ties it out and drafts the notes

Every total is checked against the statement (13 checks). It builds the two-page snapshot and suggests a note for each line that is meaningfully off budget, compared with what was explained last month.

The FA confirms, explains or removes each note

The snapshot shows up at the top of the FA's review queue. For each note they type the reason and confirm, confirm the figures as they are, or remove a note that doesn't belong. Notes explained last month are confirmed together in one click. (The previous note shown is for illustration.)

One click sends it to the PM, from the FA's own mailbox

Once every note is confirmed, the FA clicks Confirm & send. The PM gets an email with the key figures, the notes, a PDF copy and a one-time link. They have 48 hours.

No portal, no sign-in. The email button opens the full two-page snapshot on any phone or computer, with Confirm right underneath.
Or: send it back. The PM writes what to fix. It returns to the top of the FA's queue marked Changes requested, and the FA sends a new version.

The PM reads the actual report and confirms it

The PM scrolls through both pages and taps Confirm at the bottom. If something is wrong, they send it back to the FA with a note instead.

Nothing ever becomes final on its own. Only the PM's confirmation makes it final.

If the PM doesn't answer, the portal follows up

An hourly check sends one reminder at 24 hours and escalates to the FA and Jacob at 48. Silence never counts as approval.
